# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

**Primary — self-care regulars.** People who buy 金花樓 soap for their own daily use,
not as a one-off gift. Their context: choosing (or restocking) a bar for the bathroom
shelf, thinking about how it feels on the skin and the small ritual of washing. They've
moved past mass-market and want something genuinely made-by-hand and natural. Lifetime
value comes from reorders, not single conversions — so the path back to a favourite bar
must stay calm and frictionless.

**Secondary —** gift-givers (節禮 / 婚禮 / 新居 custom enquiries) and the craft-curious
who arrive through the 本舍小記 journal or Instagram.

**Context of use:** mostly mobile, in Taiwan, arriving via Instagram (@jinhuasoap) or
LINE, browsing unhurried. Traditional Chinese (zh-Hant) is the language they read in.

**Job to be done:** find the bar that fits my skin and my mood, trust that it's honestly
well-made by the two people behind it, and reorder without friction.

## Product Purpose

金花樓 is the official site of a two-person family soap studio in Linkou, Taiwan. The
wife develops and hand-presses cold-process natural soap — one recipe at a time, a batch
cured over forty-two days; the husband keeps the site and the words. The site exists to
make that slow craft *felt*, and to let people who care about what touches their skin
discover it, trust it, and come back to it.

It is deliberately **not** a high-volume store. It is a brand home where the making is
the product: the ink-wash atmosphere, the 本舍小記 journal, the twelve flowers, and a
quiet, trustworthy order form. Success looks like a visitor slowing down, feeling the
considered quality, trusting the makers — and the right bar finding them, more than once.

## Brand Personality

**Quietly premium · literary · hand-made.**

- **Quietly premium** — restraint is the luxury. Never loud, never discount-led; the
  value shows in space, materials, and care, not in announcements.
- **Literary** — the voice is first-person from 老闆娘鍋邊 (the boss-lady at her pot),
  paced with em-dashes (──), unhurried and 文人 in tone. Products are described by series
  and mood, never by performance.
- **Hand-made** — two people, no other employees; every cut, press, and wrap done by
  hand. Honesty and craft are the whole proposition.

Emotional goal: **calm confidence and considered desire** — the visitor should feel
they've found something genuine and elevated, and slow down to read it.

## Anti-references

The site must not look or feel like any of these (all four were called out explicitly):

- **Generic e-commerce.** No template product grids, no sale badges, no "Add to cart"
  Shopify-store sameness. The shop is a quiet catalogue, not a conversion funnel.
- **Drugstore / mass-market promo.** No loud discounts, no "100% 天然!" clichés, no busy
  promotional banners competing for attention.
- **Clinical / cosmetic-science.** No lab-white sterility, no ingredient-percentage
  charts, no dermatology-brand coldness. (Also a legal line: no medical or efficacy
  claims, per 化妝品法規.)
- **Trendy startup minimalism.** No big geometric sans, gradient blobs, or the
  interchangeable 2020s startup look.

Also avoid the **editorial-typographic AI reflex** (display-serif-italic + tiny mono
labels + ruled columns + uppercase eyebrows on every section). This brand's restraint is
earned from 水墨 / literati culture and Traditional Chinese typography — not borrowed from
magazine cosplay.

## Design Principles

1. **The making is the message.** Show the slow craft — the forty-two-day cure, the two
   hands, the pot — don't claim it. Atmosphere and process over performance bullets.
2. **Restraint reads as quality.** Quietly premium means generous space and few words
   well-chosen. Remove before adding; nothing should shout.
3. **Felt on the skin, not sold.** Speak to how a bar feels and the ritual of using it —
   the self-care regular's world — by series and mood, never as efficacy claims.
4. **Heritage in the voice, not the costume.** The 水墨 / literati depth lives in
   typography, imagery, and copy. Never lean on cultural decoration for its own sake.
5. **Trust over friction.** A two-person studio earns belief through honesty — real
   photos, real makers, clear shipping and order tracking — and the route to reorder
   should be the calmest path on the site.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- **Target WCAG 2.1 AA.**
- **Traditional Chinese (zh-Hant) first.** Line-height and letter-spacing tuned for CJK
  serif legibility; the EN i18n scaffold exists but is paused — zh-Hant is the shipping
  experience.
- **Contrast watch-point:** body text must clear 4.5:1 on the warm ivory paper
  (`--paper #f8f5eb`). Muted ink (`--ink-60`) on paper sits near the AA floor — bump
  toward `--sumi` for sustained reading rather than going lighter "for elegance".
- **Reduced motion is honoured.** Any ink-wash, reveal, or scroll motion needs a
  `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` fallback (crossfade or instant).
- **Mobile-first.** Most traffic is phones via Instagram / LINE; layouts collapse to a
  single column below 900px.
- **Alt text in the brand's voice** — descriptive and atmospheric, part of the writing,
  not an afterthought.
